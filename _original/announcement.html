2026-06-30

JPHD Announcement Article Processing Charge (APC) Effective January 1, 2027

The Journal of Public Health and Development (JPHD) announces that, effective for all manuscripts submitted on or after January 1, 2027, the Article Processing Charge (APC) will be USD 200 per article.

The APC is payable only after the manuscript has successfully completed the peer-review process and has received final acceptance for publication.

Refund and Return Policy

Authors are requested to note the following APC policy:

  • The APC must be paid after the manuscript has been officially accepted for publication.
  • If authors choose to withdraw their manuscript after the APC has been paid, the APC is non-refundable.
  • If a published article is retracted due to research misconduct or any form of publication malpractice, the publisher assumes no responsibility for the withdrawal or retraction, and no refund of the APC will be provided.

JPHD joins TCI-TSRI-Scopus Collaboration Project Phase 1-2

2020-08-31


Assoc. Prof. Dr. Cheerawit Rattanapan, Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Public Health and Development

and a lecturer of the ASEAN Institute for Health Development (AIHD), Mahidol University, together

with Asst. Prof. Dr. Orapin Laosee, Associate Editor of the Journal of Public Health and also a lecturer of AIHD,

Mahidol University joined the meeting of the "TCI-TSRI-Scopus Collaboration Project, Phase 1-2" organized

by the Thai-Journal Citation Index Centre (TCI) and in collaboration with the Thailand Science Research and

Innovation (TSRI), the School of Energy, Environment and Materials at King Mongkut's University of Technology

Thonburi and the National Electronics and Computer Technology Center (NECTEC) on 21 August 2020,

at the Ambassador Hotel Bangkok, Wattana, Bangkok, Thailand.


The objectives of the said meeting are to announce the project closure report of the TCI-TSRI-Scopus

Collaboration Project Phase 1, provide a report on the details and conditions of the funding of the Thai Journal

development in the TCI-TSRI-Scopus Collaboration Project Phase 2 promotions. Moreover, meeting participants

have also discussed plans to develop the quality of the Thai journal further.

Call for Manuscripts

2020-04-23

 

           Journal of Public Health and Development (JPHD) is published online focuses on providing prompt

 

publications from original research in all aspects of public health. Contributions in the form of original articles,

 

review articles and short reports are considered for publication.

 

 

         JPHD welcomes research manuscripts, reviews and reports within the scope of the contexts below.


          1. Primary health care and community health


          2. Health policy and management


          3. Health promotion and education


          4. Environmental and occupational health


          5. Addiction study and behavior


          6. Global health research

 

 

          JPHD is inviting manuscripts for online submission at https://he01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/AIHD-MU.

 

 

          For any additional information, please contact the Editor-in-Chief at cheerawit.rat@mahidol.edu
